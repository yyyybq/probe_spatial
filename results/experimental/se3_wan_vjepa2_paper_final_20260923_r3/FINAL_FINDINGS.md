# Final Findings

1. **Grounded two-step prediction is supported across the frozen multilayer set, but action-specific gain is distinct.** All 12 layers have positive scene-bootstrap gain over copy; all five V-JEPA2 layers and six of seven Wan layers also have positive gain over the without-action predictor (Wan L0 is inconclusive). See `table_main_layers.csv` and `figures/figure_multilayer.*`.

2. **The only supported predeclared cross-model difference is the representative-layer composition metric.** Wan L15 minus V-JEPA2 L11 normalized sequential--direct distance is 0.01452, 95% CI [0.01072, 0.01879]. The stored empirical/Holm value is zero because 0/2000 opposing-tail resamples occurred; with four registered Holm tests the paper reports $p_{Holm}<0.004$. Copy gain, no-action gain, and order effect do not differ reliably. This is not an overall model ranking. See `table_representative_comparison.csv` and `figures/figure_representative_comparison.*`.

3. **No robust control-adjusted order advantage is detected, and the cause is not identifiable as representation alone.** Even the high physical non-commutativity strata have CIs crossing zero (`table_noncommutativity_strata.csv`). The analytic oracle succeeds (order margin 0.1107 m), while the single-step learned GRU has one-step RMSE 0.1785 m, sequential RMSE 0.7010 m, and order margin -0.0004 m with an interval crossing zero. Thus data geometry, diagnostic-head generalization, and representation remain partly confounded; this is neither evidence of no SE(3) capability nor proof of a full group action. See `table_positive_control.csv` and `figures/figure_positive_control.*`.
