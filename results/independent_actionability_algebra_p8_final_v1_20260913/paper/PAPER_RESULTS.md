# Paper Results

## Actionability

We evaluated 227 queries from 77 validation scenes. The earlier pilot had already touched every validation scene, so these estimates characterize the frozen validation protocol rather than a fresh confirmatory test.

**DINO.** Direct accuracy was 0.762 ± 0.008 [0.709, 0.815] with regret 0.060 ± 0.002 [0.045, 0.078]; dynamics selection reached 0.762 ± 0.012 [0.703, 0.815], and the observed-endpoint reference reached 0.855 ± NA [0.810, 0.899]. The paired direct-over-random accuracy effect was +0.262 (95% CI +0.204 to +0.315; favored the first method), direct-over-action-only was +0.084 (95% CI +0.019 to +0.147; favored the first method), direct-over-goal+action/no-current was +0.001 (95% CI -0.046 to +0.048; was inconclusive), dynamics-over-direct was +0.000 (95% CI -0.050 to +0.051; was inconclusive), and the intact-over-shuffled-goal effect was +0.207 (95% CI +0.141 to +0.274; favored the first method).

**F3R.** Direct accuracy was 0.799 ± 0.013 [0.745, 0.848] with regret 0.050 ± 0.002 [0.037, 0.065]; dynamics selection reached 0.781 ± 0.003 [0.725, 0.833], and the observed-endpoint reference reached 0.780 ± NA [0.724, 0.836]. The paired direct-over-random accuracy effect was +0.299 (95% CI +0.244 to +0.348; favored the first method), direct-over-action-only was +0.120 (95% CI +0.067 to +0.175; favored the first method), direct-over-goal+action/no-current was +0.010 (95% CI -0.029 to +0.050; was inconclusive), dynamics-over-direct was -0.018 (95% CI -0.058 to +0.022; was inconclusive), and the intact-over-shuffled-goal effect was +0.175 (95% CI +0.108 to +0.242; favored the first method).

**V-JEPA2.** Direct accuracy was 0.781 ± 0.007 [0.732, 0.831] with regret 0.055 ± 0.002 [0.042, 0.070]; dynamics selection reached 0.767 ± 0.008 [0.712, 0.818], and the observed-endpoint reference reached 0.780 ± NA [0.725, 0.833]. The paired direct-over-random accuracy effect was +0.281 (95% CI +0.229 to +0.333; favored the first method), direct-over-action-only was +0.103 (95% CI +0.042 to +0.161; favored the first method), direct-over-goal+action/no-current was +0.021 (95% CI -0.019 to +0.066; was inconclusive), dynamics-over-direct was -0.015 (95% CI -0.056 to +0.029; was inconclusive), and the intact-over-shuffled-goal effect was +0.162 (95% CI +0.091 to +0.230; favored the first method).

**Wan.** Direct accuracy was 0.759 ± 0.009 [0.705, 0.809] with regret 0.060 ± 0.002 [0.045, 0.078]; dynamics selection reached 0.636 ± 0.011 [0.577, 0.692], and the observed-endpoint reference reached 0.683 ± NA [0.624, 0.739]. The paired direct-over-random accuracy effect was +0.259 (95% CI +0.210 to +0.308; favored the first method), direct-over-action-only was +0.081 (95% CI +0.029 to +0.131; favored the first method), direct-over-goal+action/no-current was -0.023 (95% CI -0.055 to +0.009; was inconclusive), dynamics-over-direct was -0.123 (95% CI -0.181 to -0.063; favored the second method), and the intact-over-shuffled-goal effect was +0.122 (95% CI +0.062 to +0.182; favored the first method).

Across all four representations, the direct scorer did not significantly outperform the goal+action/no-current control. Together with the large loss under goal shuffling, this supports goal-conditioned candidate-action selection while leaving the incremental contribution of the current visual state unresolved.
